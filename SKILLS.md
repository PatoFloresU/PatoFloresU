# Technical skills in practice

I study data science and mathematics at Tecnológico de Monterrey. These examples show how I used each method, what I evaluated, and which parts of the work I owned.

| Area | Methods and tools | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statistical research design | Within-day comparisons, fixed-effects regression, week-clustered inference, wild cluster bootstrap, Romano–Wolf multiple-testing correction, TOST equivalence testing | I wrote the analysis software for our study of Monterrey air quality. I compared early and late morning readings on the same day, controlled for zone, month and weekday, and tested whether conclusions held under alternative data treatments. [Analysis and diagnostics](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/monterrey-school-calendar-air-quality) |
| Topological data analysis | Mapper with a PCA lens and local K-means, Vietoris–Rips persistent homology (H0 and H1), persistence diagrams, Betti curves and persistence images | I represented 81 players by position-adjusted performance and compared player- and team-level structure at Worlds 2024 using KeplerMapper, NetworkX, Ripser and Persim. [Individual notebook](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/worlds-2024-topological-analysis/blob/main/Worlds_2024_Analysis.ipynb) |
| Feature engineering and model evaluation | Role-wise standardization, dimensionality reduction, group-aware and leave-one-team-out validation, baseline comparisons, linear and logistic regression | In the same project, I preserved the five positions in a 30-dimensional roster vector. I compared original, topological and combined features, and documented how player-level aggregates without match timestamps limit the interpretation of held-out scores. [Findings](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/worlds-2024-topological-analysis) |
| Neural networks and tuning | Scikit-learn multilayer perceptron, early stopping, randomized hyperparameter search, stratified cross-validation, probability thresholds, F1 and confusion matrices | In an individual notebook for a university fintech challenge, I tested how model and threshold choices affected classification of the minority class. The notebook also documents how I handled records with and without a credit history. |
| Reproducible Python analysis | pandas, NumPy, Jupyter, statsmodels, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Git, dependency management | For the air-quality paper, I connected monitoring records to school calendars and documented the analysis across 11 notebooks. In the Ternium team project, I organized three notebooks, refined the route-ranking workflow and documented how to run it. [Air-quality code](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/monterrey-school-calendar-air-quality) · [Ternium case study](Ternium_Route_Prioritization.pdf) |
| Requirements, access and privacy | Stakeholder requirements, access governance, auditability and user documentation | For the Casa Monarca academic prototype, I helped identify priority documents and external verifiers, assessed access and audit responsibilities, and co-authored the 18-page user manual. [Case study](Casa_Monarca_Digital_Identity.pdf) |
| Technical communication | Scientific writing, visual explanation of uncertainty, English-language technical presentations | I was first and corresponding author of the air-quality paper and presented it at ACESD 2026 in Tokyo, where I received the Best Presentation award. [Research brief](Monterrey_Air_Quality_Project_Brief.pdf) |

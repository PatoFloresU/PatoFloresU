## School calendars and morning air quality in Monterrey

I am the first and corresponding author of a study on a question about my city. Does the difference between early- and late-morning pollution change when school is in session? I wrote the analysis software and organized it into 11 Jupyter notebooks. We compared three years of hourly measurements from 15 monitoring stations and examined five pollutants.

The clearest result was for carbon monoxide. The early-to-late morning concentration ratio was 4.6% higher during school terms than during vacations (95% confidence interval, 1.5% to 7.7%). I presented the findings in Tokyo and received the **Best Presentation** award at ACESD 2026. The paper, co-authored with Raúl Gómez Muñoz and Blanca Rosa Ruiz Hernández, was **accepted for publication in the ACESD 2026 conference proceedings**.

[Analysis code and results](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/monterrey-school-calendar-air-quality) · [One-page research brief](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/PatoFloresU/blob/main/Monterrey_Air_Quality_Project_Brief.pdf)

## Prioritizing interplant supply routes with Ternium

Which routes should an operations team review first? In a six-person university team, we compared a model based on routine dispatch measures with one that also captured patterns across shipments. I organized three analysis notebooks, refined the workflow that turned model outputs into a route ranking, and documented the dependencies and steps my teammates could use to reproduce it.

[Read the Ternium project brief](https://github.com/PatoFloresU/PatoFloresU/blob/main/Ternium_Route_Prioritization.pdf)

## Digital identity and document verification for Casa Monarca

Our five-person team built an academic prototype for a nonprofit that supports people in migration. The goal was to let a recipient verify who signed a document while keeping unrelated records private. I helped define which documents and external verifiers to prioritize, examined access and audit responsibilities in my individual assessment, and co-authored the 18-page user manual.
